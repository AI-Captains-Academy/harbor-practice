# Code of Conduct ⚓

This is a place to learn. Everyone here started at zero. Act like it.

## The short version

- **Be kind.** Assume good faith. Ask questions without fear.
- **Help the next person up.** You were on rung 1 last week. Someone is there now.
- **Critique code, not people.** In review, be specific and generous.
- **No gatekeeping.** "That's obvious" and "just google it" are not welcome here. If it were
  obvious, they wouldn't be asking.
- **Celebrate first PRs.** A merged first PR is a big deal. Treat it like one.

## Not okay

Harassment, insults, discrimination, or making anyone feel small for not knowing something
yet. Doing this gets your contributions closed and, if it continues, your access removed.

## Reporting

Something's off? Bring it to a maintainer directly in the Harbor community, or email
ahoy@aicaptains.ai. We'll handle it quietly and fairly.

Welcome aboard. Build cool things. Be good to each other. ⚓
