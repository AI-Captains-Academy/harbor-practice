## What goes in each column

**Time to live** — How long it takes to go from "I want to host this" to "a stranger can host it in the browser."  Write a real number, not the marketing number. (10+ min is honest. "5 minutes" usually isn't.)

**$/month** — your actual cost, not the list price. Level 1 is $0. Level 2 on an hourly-billed VPS is pennies if you destroy it after the lab. Level 3 is electricity — measure it from your utility bill, not a guess. All prices: verify at publish.

**What breaks it** — the one thing that takes this deployment offline. Power cut? Provider outage? You forgot to pay? A domain expiry? Name the single failure mode that scares you most for each rung... (Ask me how I know. Every one of these has happened to me.)

**Who can take it down** — this maps to the *trust spectrum*. Who holds the kill switch? At Level 1, the platform (GitHub/Netlify) can suspend you. At Level 2, the VPS provider can. At Level 3... you and your utility company — and if you use a Cloudflare tunnel to make it reachable, Cloudflare too. The tunnel hides your IP, but it adds a landlord. Write every name. Be honest about who you're trusting.

**Who sees your IP** — whose server log shows your home IP address? Level 1: nobody (traffic goes to GitHub/Netlify). Level 2: nobody (traffic goes to the VPS). Level 3 with a tunnel: nobody (Cloudflare sits in front). Without a tunnel: everyone. This column is why the tunnel lesson (2.2) exists.

## How to use this

Don't fill it all in at once. Each lesson tells you which row to update:

- **Lesson 1.1** → Level 1 row (time + cost)
- **Lesson 1.2** → Level 2 row (all five cells after the break-it lab)
- **Lesson 2.1** → Level 3 cost cell
- **Lesson 2.2** → Level 3 remaining cells (who sees your IP, per rung)
- **Lesson 2.5** → complete every cell from your own deployments, then write the decision memo

By the capstone, you won't have a table of facts. You'll have a table of *experience*. That's the difference between reading about self-hosting and knowing which rung you belong on.
