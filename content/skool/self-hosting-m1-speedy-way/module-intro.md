# Module 1: The Speedy Way — Rent a VPS

Most people think self-hosting means a server rack humming in their closet.

It doesn't. The fastest path to "I control everything" starts on someone else's machine — and that's not a compromise. It's the smart move.

This module is the *speedy way*. Rent a VPS, get something running today, and decide later whether you ever need more.

You'll be live with a secure HTTPS site in a single evening.
