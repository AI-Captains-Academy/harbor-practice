# Module 2: The Real Thing — Host at Home

The *real* self-hosting. Build a mini data center at home.

A fun hobby that never ends... and I'll be honest about that upfront, because the fun part and the never-ending part are the same thing.

This is where sovereignty gets physical — your own AI agents running 24/7 on hardware you own, hidden behind a Cloudflare tunnel so the world can reach your work without ever seeing your home IP.
